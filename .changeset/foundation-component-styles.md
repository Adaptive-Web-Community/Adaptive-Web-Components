The styles of the button, the accordion, and the accordion item are in the foundation, without a framework: their anatomy, their style rules, their template and aesthetic CSS as strings, and the shared `componentBaseStyles`, `svgIconStyles`, `visuallyHidden`, `tokens`, and `tokenVar`. The FAST components re-export them and wrap the CSS in `ElementStyles`.
